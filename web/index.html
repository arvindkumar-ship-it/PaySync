Every payment has a story.

Follow it from either side.

    Start with a payment

    Choose an amount and a journey. Instant confirmation settles immediately; delayed credit waits for a merchant confirmation.

    Resolve with confidence

    A debited payment without credit moves to refund pending after the timeout. Check the retry decision, create a separate payment, then simulate the original refund.
